PATCH NOTES — AI/ADAPTIVE FIX 2026-09-21

Targeted fixes only:
1. AI Tutor now receives a compact story context from the whole E-Comic: episode order, panel narration/dialogue, characters, equations, concepts, plus the active panel image when available.
2. Tutor prompt changed to a natural, active, conversational learning style. It may reference characters/story events when supported and must not invent details.
3. Tutor fallback is conversational instead of repeating generic "Mari kita tetap gunakan konteks belajar..." text. Server now retries text/story context when panel-image AI processing fails.
4. Practice: "Tanya Tutor AI" is locked until the student has failed the current question 3 times.
5. Practice: after a correct answer, the next question is generated by AI as a new question, not merely selected from the bank. Difficulty uses hints/failed attempts and only eligible/previously learned concepts.
6. Removed the old background AI recommendation call after every answer to reduce unnecessary AI load and latency.
7. AI correction prompt now explicitly makes the AI determine misconception from evidence, and distinguishes conceptual misconception from a simple calculation/click error.
8. AI correction/generation timeouts and output sizes were reduced to improve responsiveness while keeping AI evaluation active.

The existing Firebase, comic editor, attendance, analytics, mastery, exam and other features were not intentionally rewritten.
